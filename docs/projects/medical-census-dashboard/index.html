Medical Census Analytics Dashboard

A Tableau dashboard of 400k+ state-wide medical census data points, broken down by demographics and geography, with an ETL pipeline in progress.

Status
Completed
Date
Jul 2026
Built with
Tableau, ETL, Data Analytics, Data Viz

During my summer internship at Blue Cross Blue Shield of Mississippi, I built a Tableau dashboard on more than 400,000 state-wide medical census data points. It makes trends easy to see and lets you break them down by demographics and geography.

Some data is left out. The screenshots hide details such as field names, and the county map is a placeholder that covers the actual distribution of members.

The dashboard

I put the main views of the census on a single screen: unique patients and readmissions, how long stays last, monthly hospital stays with the share that were readmissions, how many different hospitals members visited, a county map of visits, admissions by level of care, a breakdown by primary diagnosis, and the age distribution.

The data pipeline

Behind the dashboard I’m building an ETL pipeline that automates ingesting, cleaning, and transforming the incoming census data before it reaches Tableau. One job in it fixes up admission and discharge dates and adds the fields the dashboard depends on, including a stay ID and a flag for readmissions.
